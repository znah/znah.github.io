Ruliobiology

Growing creatures from tiny rules

How a table of colored squares can grow shapes that look alive — and how “evolution” can find amazing ones all by itself.

How a new row is made

We start with one colored square. Each square in the next row looks at three squares above it: the one to the left, the one right above, and the one to the right. Then it finds that pattern in the rule table and copies the answer.

Mutation: change one answer

A mutation is a tiny change: pick one pattern in the table and give it a different answer (its mirror twin changes too). Sometimes nothing happens… sometimes the creature changes completely!

What score? We can choose what we want the creature to be good at:

⏳ Lifetime

How many rows it grows before it disappears. Long-lived creatures!

↔ Width

How wide it gets. Big, spreading creatures!

⚖️ Mass

How many colored squares it has. Chunky creatures!

😴 Lazy

What share of its colored squares just copy the color right above them. It must live at least 20 rows. Calm, striped creatures!

One rule: creatures that never stop growing are disqualified — they must finish!
